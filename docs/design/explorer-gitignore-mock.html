Show ignored files, right in the explorer.

One button beside the repository name. Ignored files stay hidden by default; click the eye to show them. Click again to hide them.

Ignored files hidden · default

Off: the eye is neutral. Tooltip: “Show ignored files”. .gitignore itself stays visible.

Ignored files visible

On: the eye turns blue. Tooltip: “Hide ignored files”. Ignored entries appear in their usual positions.
