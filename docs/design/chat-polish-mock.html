S4Tool calls grouped

Today every tool call is its own bordered row. A turn with 30 calls is 30 rows, and the agent's words get lost between them.

  • One block per run. The tool calls and thinking between two pieces of the agent's text fold into one block.

What runs right now is said once, by the working line of S5 just below. Opening the line shows every row, the running one with the still working mark.

S6Attachments: paste images, drop files

Today pasting an image or dropping a file on a chat does nothing. Only terminals take them.

  • Drop files anywhere on the chat pane. While they are dragged over it, the pane shows "Drop to attach" with a paperclip, an accent wash and a solid 2px accent border, as the terminal's drop hint.
  • Paste an image, or copied files, into the composer to attach them. Pasted text is still text.
  • Chips in the composer, in a row above the text. An image is a 44px rounded thumbnail and its name; a file is a file icon, its name and size. Each has an x to remove it, on hover and focus.
  • On a remote host each chip shows its upload: a thin bar along its bottom, then a check for a moment. Send is off, with the tooltip "Uploading…", until every upload has finished. A failed upload turns its chip to the danger colour with Retry and Remove. On this machine nothing uploads, so there is no bar.
  • Sent, your bubble shows the same chips above its text. Clicking an image opens it in the app's viewer; clicking a file opens the file.
  • Limits: images up to 25 MiB, the existing upload limit, and at most 10 attachments per message. Past either, the notice line over the composer says why.

What differs

  • T3's dark theme is neutral black; Muxflow's is the blue-grey One Dark ground. The light grounds are nearly the same.
  • T3 separates the user's bubble by fill alone, a much bigger step from the pane (1.26 against 1.07 in dark), and no border. Muxflow uses a small fill step and a hairline.
  • T3's tool rows are bare lines with no box. Muxflow boxes each one; S4 already removes most boxes by grouping.
  • T3's composer is a raised surface lighter than the pane. Muxflow's is the pane's colour with an outline.
  • T3's body text is its strongest ink. Muxflow's body text is a step under its strong ink.
  • Faint text is under 4.5:1 in both apps. Muxflow also has three coloured-text cases under 4.5: green and amber in light, red in dark.

Today the host drops what the agent says about work outside the turn. A command left running in the background shows nothing once the turn ends. A subagent that works for minutes is one unchanging row. While the agent compacts its context the chat looks stuck.
