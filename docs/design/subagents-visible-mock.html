Background subagents, visible

A subagent sent to the background works for many minutes, but today it is one row in the "background tasks" list, behind a chip, next to a dev server. Background commands can stay as they are. Subagents should be visible without a click.

  • Background subagents get their own tray on top of the composer, one row each, while they run. This holds whether the turn is idle or working.
  • Each row is the S9 subagent row: the bot icon with the working mark, its type and description, its time, and a second line with what it does now, tool count and tokens. There is a stop x on hover when the task is stoppable. Clicking a row scrolls to where it was started in the conversation.
  • The chip keeps the rest: it counts commands only, "1 background command", and goes away when there are none. Nothing is listed twice.
  • Four or more agents: three rows, then "Show 2 more". The tray never grows taller than four rows.
  • When one ends, its row shows check "done" for 3 seconds and then leaves. The agent's next turn says what it found, as today.
  • Subagents the turn waits on (not backgrounded) stay under the working line, as S9 has them. The tray is only for work that runs alongside you.

No wire change. The host already keeps a backgrounded subagent's own row open after its tool call returns (detached in claude.rs). That row has background = true, status RUNNING, and live activity, tool_uses and tokens from task_progress. The tray is items filtered on that, drawn on the desktop only. Mobile is unaffected.
