Phone chat: one mic for dictation and voice mode

Today a chat has two buttons that do the same thing. The headphones in the header and the mic in the reply field both turn voice mode on. Voice mode then swaps the reply field for a slim bar. That bar has no Lock or Discard targets, no Autoplay switch, and puts the speed on the reply instead of with the other controls.

Proposed: the headphones go, and the mic in the reply field does two things.

  • Hold to dictate one message. Release and it is transcribed and sent, without entering voice mode.
  • Tap to open voice mode, a conversation where replies are read aloud. It gets the Voice screen's talk pane with the left and right swipe targets, plus Speed, Autoplay and a way back to the keyboard.

The phone screens are drawings in the app's colours. The ones marked Now are drawn from the code. Nothing is implemented yet. Answer the questions at the bottom, then press Copy answers and paste the result to Claude.

LeaveThe keyboard button. Back leaves the chat as it does now.
  • Tap and hold apart. A press shorter than a quarter of a second is a tap and opens voice mode. Held longer, recording starts, and a haptic tick says it is listening. The microphone is readied when the finger goes down, never just because a chat opened: readying it takes the audio session (other apps duck, a headset switches to its call profile) and asks for the permission the first time.
  • The keyboard open. Holding the mic leaves the keyboard up, and the strip takes the field's place. Tapping the mic closes the keyboard and opens voice mode.
  • Voice not set up on the host. Tapping the mic opens voice mode with the "Set up voice" card, and the text field stays, as now. Its mic shows that voice mode is on, and a tap on it turns voice mode off. Holding records nothing and says "Voice isn't set up on this host" in a toast.
  • A question or permission card waiting, or an agent that takes no message while it works. Holding records nothing and says why. In voice mode the card stays above the pane and is answered by tapping.
  • The Agents tab's Talk still opens a chat straight into voice mode.
  • The terminal Voice screen keeps its layout. Its Bigger/Smaller switch does not come to the chat, because the pane is already a third of the screen.

What changes in the code

  • ChatHeader: the headphones button goes.
  • Composer: a tap on the mic turns voice mode on, and a hold drives the chat's VoiceController, which already sends a transcript with chatClient.send. The recording strip is new.
  • Voice mode uses the talk pane: the Voice screen's controls row (Speed, Autoplay) becomes a shared component, and the MicButton pane variant moves in. The bar variant and ChatVoiceBar are deleted, and the reply row loses its speed button.
  • useChatVoice: replies are read aloud only in voice mode, and the chat follows the Autoplay setting instead of always playing.
  • The host and the protocol do not change.
